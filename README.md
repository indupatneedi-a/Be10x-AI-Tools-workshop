 🚀 be10X AI Tools Workshop

A collection of my learning notes, resources, and practical takeaways from the **be10X AI Tools Workshop — Become 10X: Boosting Your Performance at Work**.

The workshop focused on using modern AI tools to improve productivity, research, content creation, coding, data analysis, career preparation, and workflow automation.

## 📌 About the Workshop

The workshop introduced a range of AI-powered tools and demonstrated how they can be applied to real-world professional tasks.

The major areas covered included:

* 🔎 AI-powered research
* 📝 AI-assisted writing and productivity
* 🎨 AI image and creative generation
* 💻 Website and application development
* 📊 Data analysis and visualization
* 📄 Resume optimization
* 🎤 Interview preparation
* 💼 AI-assisted job discovery
* ⚙️ Workflow automation
* 🤖 Agentic AI and AI-powered personal assistants

## 🛠️ Tools Explored

### 1. Grok

Used for **deep research** and generating detailed, structured reports with references.

### 2. Get Multi

Explored for comparing responses from multiple AI models simultaneously and selecting the most useful output.

### 3. Wispr Flow AI

Explored AI-powered voice dictation for faster, hands-free writing and productivity.

### 4. NotebookLM

Explored for working with multiple sources such as documents, audio, video, YouTube links, and text, along with generating summaries, mind maps, infographics, quizzes, and audio/video overviews.

### 5. Gemini

Explored for AI-powered visual creation and website development, including generating website interfaces and code from prompts and images.

### 6. Google AI Studio

Explored for building applications using Google's latest AI models and creating applications through natural-language prompts.

### 7. Julius AI

Explored for analyzing datasets, generating insights, creating visualizations, and producing dashboards and presentations.

### 8. 10X Resume Hack

Explored for improving resumes for ATS compatibility and comparing resume scores against job descriptions.

### 9. ChatGPT

Explored for real-time mock interview practice using the voice feature and role-based interview questions.

### 10. Comet

Explored for AI-assisted job discovery and finding opportunities based on a user's profile and requirements.

### 11. n8n

Explored for **workflow automation, application integration, and agentic AI workflows**. The workshop demonstrated an AI personal assistant connected with tools such as Telegram, Gmail, Google Calendar, Fireflies, Google Search, and an OpenAI model.
